Our 5 Step Process

Our Proven 5-Step Process: Building Your Dream Home With Ease

Experience A Seamless, Stress-Free, And Personalised Home Building Journey

Our expert team is dedicated to providing a seamless, stress-free, and personalised homebuilding experience. With our proven 5-step process, you'll be guided through every stage of the journey, from initial consultation to final handover. Discover the Sena Constructions difference and let us build the perfect home that reflects your unique style and needs.

1

Step 1 - Initial Consultation And Property Assessment

Connect with our team to discuss your vision, preferences, and budget for your dream home. We'll review any property documents you have and gather relevant information to assess the feasibility of your project. This step will help us understand your unique requirements and set the stage for a successful collaboration.

2

Step 2 - Site Visit And Customised Proposal

Our consultants will visit your property to examine site-specific factors that might affect the construction process. We'll discuss your preferred materials, design choices, and budget constraints to create a detailed, fixed-price proposal tailored to your needs. Our in-house team will work closely with you to fine-tune the proposal and ensure it aligns with your vision.

3

Step 3 - Design Development And Material Selection

Once you approve the proposal, we'll collaborate with our architects and designers to create a custom home design that meets your requirements. You'll be involved in the design process, making any necessary changes to ensure you're completely satisfied. Simultaneously, we'll guide you through selecting the perfect materials and finishes that reflect your style & complement your home.

4

Step 4 - Approvals And Contract Preparation

We'll take care of obtaining all required approvals, including architectural plans, engineering reports, and construction certificates. While we handle the paperwork, you can focus on making final decisions about material selections and finishes. Once all approvals are secured, we'll prepare a comprehensive building contract for you to review and sign.

5

Step 5 - Construction And Handover

With approvals and contracts in place, our construction team will bring your dream home to life. Your assigned site manager will be your point of contact throughout the construction process, ensuring that your home is built on time and with the highest quality standards. Upon completion, we'll conduct a thorough inspection, rectify any defects, and hand over the keys to your new, beautifully crafted home.
