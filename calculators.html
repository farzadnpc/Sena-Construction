What affects building costs in Sydney?

Whether you're planning a new home, a duplex or a renovation, the same handful of factors make the biggest difference to your budget. Our calculators take each of them into account.

Want to learn more about a specific project type? Explore our duplex builds, knockdown rebuilds, luxury homes and renovations and extensions.

Your site

Sloping blocks need more excavation and retaining. Knockdown rebuilds include demolition, and bushfire-prone land may need BAL-rated materials.

Size and layout

Floor area, storeys and the number of bedrooms and bathrooms all add to the cost. Wet areas like bathrooms cost more per square metre than living spaces.

Finish level

Inclusions such as benchtops, tiles, joinery and fixtures can change a budget significantly. Each calculator lets you compare finish levels.

Compliance

NSW requirements like BASIX, waterproofing to AS 3740 and HBCF insurance for work over $20,000 are part of building in Sydney.
