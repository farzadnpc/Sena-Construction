About Sena Constructions

Building A Legacy Of Excellence

Sena Constructions was founded by Alisena Zafari with a vision to redefine customer expectations and experiences in the Sydney construction industry. With a strong focus on sustainability, fairness, and outstanding customer experiences, our mission is to deliver quality homes within client timeframes and budgets.

Alisena's passion for building and exceptional family history in the construction industry has established Sena Constructions as a reliable and trusted partner for all your building needs.
