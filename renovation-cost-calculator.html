    Renovation cost FAQs

    Each room starts from a base rate for its size and finish level, adjusted for Sydney labour and materials costs. Wet areas include waterproofing, lead-free fittings and an allowance for copper prices. We then show a planning range around the total, because every home is different.
    In NSW, Home Building Compensation Fund (HBCF) insurance is required for residential building work over $20,000 (including GST). Your builder arranges the cover before work starts. Check current requirements with icare NSW.
    Every bathroom needs demolition, plumbing, electrical work and waterproofing regardless of size. Those fixed costs are spread over fewer square metres in a small bathroom or ensuite.
